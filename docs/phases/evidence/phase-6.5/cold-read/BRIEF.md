# Cold-read brief (Phase 6.5 step 8, doc §5.4)

Pasted verbatim, as the first and only message, into a new Claude in Chrome conversation in his browser, with the
local preview open in a new tab. The agent is given nothing else. Everything between the two rules below is the brief.

---

Please read the website at http://127.0.0.1:4173/ the way an interested visitor would. Start at that address and
follow whatever links you like, for as long as you find useful. Read the pages; you do not need to change anything
on them or fill anything in.

When you are done, report back in your own words:

1. What was tested.
2. What was found.
3. What it means.

For each point, say which page or pages you got it from. Then list the pages you read, and anything you found
unclear, hard to follow, or that you were unsure how to interpret.
